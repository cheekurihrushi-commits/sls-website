Arena

Where Determination Meets Team Spirit.

🏃

The SLS Annual Sports Meet. Classmates become teammates. Houses become competitors. Practice becomes performance. Every finish line becomes a lesson in determination.

Run Faster. Aim Higher. Cheer Louder. Play With Pride.

The Arena Torch — one flame, four houses, one SLS. Passed through student leaders, it marks the beginning of Arena: compete with courage, win with humility, lose with dignity.

SCIQUEST

Where Curiosity Becomes Discovery.

🔬

Science becomes exciting when students can see ideas come alive. Through SCIQUEST, students explore scientific concepts through models, experiments, demonstrations and creative projects. They don't simply read about how something works — they build it, test it, explain it.

Celebrating Their First Milestone.

For our young learners, graduation is more than a cap, certificate and photograph — it celebrates their first important educational journey. From entering the classroom with tiny steps to standing proudly on the graduation stage, they have already come a long way. And their journey is only beginning.

Learning to Care

Education Has a Heart.

Through environmental initiatives, value-based activities, teamwork and community participation, students are encouraged to practise kindness, respect, responsibility, compassion and environmental awareness.

Because knowledge makes students capable. Values make them human.

For some, it's holding their first medal. For some, it's answering a question they once found difficult. For some, it's hearing applause after their first performance. For some, it's being elected by their classmates. For some, it's seeing their science project finally work. And for some, it's simply finding the courage to participate.

Our job is to create enough opportunities for every child to find theirs.
