About SLS Scholars School

A school built around structure, character, and genuine student leadership.

Our Story

Rooted in Suryapet, built for the long run.

SLS Scholars School was founded with a simple belief: children learn best in a structured environment where they carry real responsibility — not just for their studies, but for their community. That's why every student at SLS belongs to one of four houses from day one, and why leadership positions in the school are earned through actual student elections, not appointed by staff.

Today, SLS Scholars School serves over 500 students from Nursery through Class X, with a dedicated teaching staff of more than 35, and a curriculum that extends into IIT foundation coursework for our senior grades.

Message from the Correspondent & Principal

"Every child who walks through our gates carries potential we haven't discovered yet. Our job isn't just to teach a syllabus — it's to give every student a house to belong to, a role to grow into, and the discipline to carry both forward."

— Correspondent & Principal, SLS Scholars School

What We Stand For

Our Houses & Their Meaning

Each house is named for a value we want every student to carry with them long after they graduate.

Sathya

Truth — honesty in word and action.

Daya

Compassion — kindness toward others.

Saralatha

Simplicity — humility in character.

Pavithratha

Purity — integrity of purpose.
Student Leadership

Elected, not appointed.

School Pupil Leader

The top student leadership role in the school, elected by the entire student body each year.

Group Captains & Vice Captains

Captains from Class X and Vice Captains from Class IX lead each of the four houses, elected by their peers.

Squad Leaders

Every class-house group is led by its top academic performer, recognised each exam term.
