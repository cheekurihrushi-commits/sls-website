2001 — 2026

25 Years of Learning. Leadership. Legacy.

Twenty-five years ago, a journey began — built classroom by classroom, teacher by teacher, student by student, family by family. Today, we celebrate not simply how long SLS has existed, but everything that has happened because it did.

25 Years. Thousands of Memories. One SLS Family.

For 25 years, SLS Scholars School has been part of children's lives — their first classrooms, their friendships, their achievements, their challenges, their celebrations, their dreams. Some arrived holding their parents' hands. Today, many are building lives and careers of their own. Yet each became part of the same continuing story.

The SLS Story.

It Started in 2001

With a Vision.

SLS Scholars School began with a belief in the power of education to shape not only knowledgeable students, but capable and responsible human beings. From those early years, the school grew through the dedication of educators, the trust of parents and the achievements of its students.

Twenty-five years later, that vision continues. The classrooms may look different. Technology may have changed. A new generation may now fill the corridors. But the purpose remains.

Educate. Encourage. Empower.

A Letter to Our Alumni

You Will Always Be an SLS Scholar.

You may no longer walk through these corridors every morning. Your classroom may belong to another batch now. Your favourite teacher may have retired. Your friends may live in different cities — perhaps different countries. But somewhere inside these walls is a younger version of you — learning, laughing, making mistakes, dreaming about what comes next.

Twenty-five years gives us a reason to look back. And when we do, we remember you.

Wherever life has taken you, welcome back to SLS.

To Today's Students

You Are Living Tomorrow's Memories.

The classroom that feels ordinary today may become the room you remember twenty years from now. The classmates sitting beside you may become lifelong friends. The teacher reminding you to finish your work may someday become someone you wish you could thank again.

Enjoy these days. Learn from them. Make them count. One day, you will be the legacy.

The Next 25

Our Story Doesn't End Here.

Twenty-five years gives us a reason to celebrate the past. But education always asks us to look forward. New technologies will emerge. New careers will be created. New challenges will confront the next generation. SLS will continue to evolve with the world while remaining grounded in the values on which it was built.

Stronger Learning Greater Opportunities Confident Communication Responsible Leadership Technology With Purpose Values That Endure

2001 gave us a beginning. 2026 gives us a milestone. The future gives us a responsibility.

Thank You for 25 Years.

To every student. Every alumnus. Every teacher. Every parent. Every staff member. Every family. Every person who believed in SLS.

Thank you for becoming part of our story.
