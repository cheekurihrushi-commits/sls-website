Academics

Nursery through Class X, with a foundation that carries into competitive exams.

Grade Levels

Every stage, one campus.

Nursery – UKG

Early years foundation — play-based learning, language, and motor skills.

Classes I – V

Core subjects: English, Telugu, Hindi, Maths, EVS — building strong fundamentals.

Classes VI – VIII

Science, Social Studies, and Maths deepen, with IIT foundation periods introduced.

Classes IX – X

Board exam preparation alongside full IIT foundation coursework.

IIT Foundation Program

Competitive exam prep, without leaving campus.

From Class VI onward, students attend dedicated IIT foundation periods built directly into the daily timetable — Physics, Chemistry, and Biology taught with an eye toward NEET and IIT-JEE readiness, alongside the regular board curriculum. No extra travel, no separate coaching centre.

A Typical School Day

Structured, with room to breathe.

8 Regular Periods

Four periods each in the morning and afternoon session, with a lunch break in between.

Extended Day for Senior Grades

Classes VIII–X get extra early and late periods to fit in additional subjects and IIT foundation classes.

Clubs & Activities

Green Club, Sports Club, and English Club — each led by an elected student leader.
