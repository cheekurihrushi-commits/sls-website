IIT Foundation Programme

Start Early. Think Bigger.

From Class VI onward, students attend dedicated IIT Foundation periods built directly into the daily timetable — Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics taught with an eye toward long-term analytical thinking, alongside the regular board curriculum. Weekly assessments track progress with subject-wise scores, class rank and percentile, so every student — and every parent — can see real growth over time.
