Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions From Parents

Which classes are admissions available for?

Admissions are offered from Nursery to Class X, subject to seat availability and applicable admission requirements.

How can I enquire about admission?

Parents can submit the online enquiry form, contact the school directly, use WhatsApp, or visit the campus.

Can we visit the school before taking admission?

Yes. We encourage parents to visit the campus and understand the SLS learning environment before making their decision.

Does SLS offer IIT Foundation?

Yes — for Classes VI–X, designed to strengthen Mathematics, Science, logical reasoning and analytical thinking.

What documents are required?

Our admissions team will provide the current list of required documents based on the class for which admission is sought.

How can I know the fee structure?

Please contact our admissions team for the applicable fee details and payment information for the academic year — or see our fee structure below.
